## Product description and sales context

PS portable balances are easy to take with you wherever your need to weigh is. Whether it's transported back and forth from school in a student's backpack or brought to different jobs in a worker's tool kit, PS makes portable weighing convenient. PS offers a simple two-button operation, a crisp LCD display and most importantly, fast and reliable results.

**Tag line:** High Performance Portable Weighing in the Palm of Your Hand!

### Source-listed benefits

- **One-piece pan slides to protect buttons:** The one-piece combination pan and cover slides back when being used to weigh and forward when not in use to protect the buttons and display during storage and transportation.
- **PS fits easily in a standard shirt pocket:** One of the smallest portable balances on the market, PS can easily fit in to a standard shirt pocket.
- **Pop-out design for easy battery change:** The "pop-out" design of the battery compartment provides simple and fast battery replacement.
